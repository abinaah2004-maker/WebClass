01 · Reading comprehension

Read for the main idea, the writer's purpose, cause and effect, and implied meaning. Try not to translate every word.

02 · Listening simulation

Listen to the Korean conversation using your browser's Korean text-to-speech voice. First listen without reading the transcript, then answer the question. This is a TTS practice simulation, not an official TOPIK audio recording.

03 · Extended writing

Write connected Korean, not isolated sentences. Aim for clear structure: situation → reason or evidence → suggestion → conclusion.

Useful connectors: 우선, 게다가, 반면에, 따라서, 이로 인해, 예를 들어, 결국, 무엇보다도.

0 charactersSuggested: 200–300 Korean characters
Self-check before saving: Did you answer every part? Did you use at least three connectors? Are your verb endings consistent? Did you explain why, not just what?

04 · Unscripted conversation room

You get a situation and a Korean person's question. Answer naturally in Korean without copying a prepared dialogue. This page gives rule-based feedback on useful structures; it is not a generative AI tutor.

05 · Intermediate vocabulary for life in Korea

Learn these in context and make your own sentence for each one.

Recommended weekly routine: Monday: one reading passage and vocabulary. Tuesday: listen twice, first without transcript. Wednesday: write 200–300 characters. Thursday: roleplay aloud and rewrite your answer. Friday: redo the weakest exercise without looking at notes. Save your work on the same browser to keep your drafts.
