01 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity into creation.

I am building my foundation in software and web development. My goal is to understand how things work, not just make a page that looks finished.

At Gachon University, I study Computer Engineering and keep developing my skills through coursework and independent practice. I enjoy building useful interfaces, working with JavaScript and learning how websites connect to real data.

Every project gives me a chance to practise problem-solving: planning the structure, writing the code, testing the result and fixing what does not work as expected.
