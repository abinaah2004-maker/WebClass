Simple vegetable fried rice

A practice page for nested HTML lists.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Vegetables
    • Carrot
    • Green onion
    • Peas
  • Oil and seasoning

Steps

  1. Prepare and chop the vegetables.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan.
  3. Cook the vegetables, then add rice.
  4. Stir, season, and serve hot.
