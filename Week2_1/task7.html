Korean Kimchi Fried Rice Recipe

What You Need

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Kimchi
  • 1 egg
  • Green onion
  • Sesame oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the cooked rice and chop the kimchi.
  2. Cook the kimchi in a pan for a few minutes.
  3. Warning: Use medium heat to avoid burning the kimchi.
  4. Add the rice and mix everything together.
  5. Fry an egg separately.
  6. Add the egg on top and serve with sesame oil.
