Manual checks to complete

A Lighthouse score covers automated checks only. Do not mark these items complete until you have tested the deployed page yourself.

  • Keyboard navigationUse Tab and Shift+Tab to reach controls in a sensible order.
  • Skip linkActivate the skip link and confirm focus reaches the main content.
  • Visible focusCheck that links, buttons and game controls show a clear focus indicator.
  • Game controlsTry the difficulty, mode, cards and restart controls with the keyboard.
  • Small screensCheck the layout at a narrow viewport and browser zoom.
  • Assistive technologyReview labels and status messages with a screen reader where available.
